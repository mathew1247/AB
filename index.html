A Smarter Way to Prepare for Technical Interviews

This section explains what InterviewAI is and why it exists.

InterviewAI is an AI-powered technical interview platform designed to simulate realistic interview experiences through adaptive conversations.

Unlike traditional interview preparation platforms that rely on fixed question lists, InterviewAI understands candidate responses and dynamically generates relevant follow-up questions.

The goal is simple:

Practice realistically. Understand your performance. Improve with every interview.
